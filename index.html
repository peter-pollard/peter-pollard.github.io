YouTube Tools

A personal channel-management tool for Everyday Microsoft 365, operated by Peter Pollard.

What the tool does

  • Researches public YouTube videos and channels through Google's official APIs.
  • Displays authorized channel inventory and available performance analytics in a local dashboard.
  • Uploads completed videos as Unlisted for review.
  • Publishes reviewed videos only after the channel owner approves the finished videos and release timing.

The operational dashboard runs on the owner's computer. This website provides information about the tool and its privacy practices; it does not offer account sign-in or access to the dashboard.

Google authorization

The owner chooses which Google permissions to grant. Upload and publishing permissions are separate from approval of an individual video. Access can be revoked in Google Account settings.
